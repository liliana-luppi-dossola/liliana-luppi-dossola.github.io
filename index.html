The Artist

Liliana Luppi Dossola

Liliana Luppi Dossola paints the fleeting moments when light transforms the ordinary — a lake at late afternoon, sun through a window onto spring flowers, a garden statue caught in the glow of midday. Working in acrylic, she builds her scenes with rich, layered color and confident brushwork, and she often composes poems to accompany her paintings, giving each image an additional voice.

Trained in commercial art and illustration, she graduated summa cum laude from NOVA, and also studied at East Tennessee State University and the Maryland Institute College of Art. Her career has spanned solo exhibitions, juried shows, public murals, curatorial work and leadership in the arts communities of Northern Virginia and Southern California.
